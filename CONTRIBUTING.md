# Contributing

ZTG distinguishes contribution from adoption. A proposal may inform the specification
without becoming part of the official specification.

## Before contributing

Open an issue describing the proposed change, the problem it addresses, and the
affected sections. Preserve the provenance of any evidence or text included with the
proposal.

Do not submit normative specification text until the normative-contribution agreement
and intake mechanism are published. OWFa 1.0 licenses the published specification; the
separate contributor agreement needed for inbound normative text remains under legal
review.

## Deployment Guide contributions

Guide contributions may be submitted by pull request. By submitting guide material,
you represent that you have the right to contribute it and agree that it may be
published under the ZTG Documentation License 1.0. Identify third-party material and
its license explicitly.

## Schema and tooling contributions

Schema and tooling contributions may be submitted by pull request under the
Apache-2.0 inbound-equals-outbound model. By submitting, you represent that you have the
right to contribute the work under Apache-2.0.

## Review and adoption

Every contribution must preserve contributor identity, authorship, date, and license
compatibility. Maintainer review and merge do not by themselves adopt normative text.
Normative changes require a `ZTG-AMEND-NNN` record and the process in
[`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

Contributing does not transfer control of the official ZTG identity, names, marks, or
conformance claims.
